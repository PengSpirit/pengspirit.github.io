  • Industry impact: most "company brain" tools assume an enterprise buyer with a data team; Acortia targets the much larger population of solo and small AI-native teams that enterprise RAG tools ignore.

Founder Mindset

I have no engineering background. That constraint forced me to clarify my thinking — every product decision must be defensible to a non-technical founder (me). My obsessions:

  • Unit economics — not vanity metrics.
  • Vertical slices — ship one thing end-to-end before breadth.
  • Operational clarity — every step must be executable by a non-engineer.
  • Shipping speed — constraint forces ruthless prioritization.
